♡ page 04 · words just for you

To the One I Keep Choosing.

Dear Jo,

Getting to know you has made me realize that loving someone is also about discovering the parts of them that aren't always easy to understand. I know there's still so much for me to learn about you, and I don't expect us to figure everything out overnight. I want to know you beyond the happy moments, to understand where you're coming from, and to make you feel that you never have to hide a part of yourself just to be loved by me.

I know we may not see eye to eye sometimes. We might have different ways of thinking, misunderstand each other, or struggle to find the right words. But I never want a disagreement to make us forget why we chose each other. I'd rather listen, meet you halfway, and work through things with you than let pride get in the way.
I know we're both still learning, and I want to keep doing that together—not perfectly, but sincerely.

And even with the distance between us, I hope you can feel that I'm genuinely here, not only when things are easy, but also when life gets overwhelming. I want to be someone you can talk to without worrying about being judged, someone who celebrates your wins and listens when things don't go as planned. I may not always know the perfect thing to say, but I'll always want to understand what you need and how I can love you better.

Love,
Gabby

On Your Hard Days,
Remember This. ♡

You don't have to have everything figured out to be loved.
You can be honest with me, even on the days when things feel heavy.

🌸 Tell me the truth. I would rather hear what is really going on than have you suffer quietly because you are afraid of being too much
🧸 You are allowed to rest. You do not owe me constant replies, a cheerful mood, or proof of love every hour of the day.
💌 Let us ask instead of assume. If something feels wrong, I want us to talk about it before fear fills in the blanks for us.
🫶 We can have a hard day without making each other the enemy. A difficult moment deserves care, not a scorecard of who is right.

You never have to earn kindness
from me by being easy to love. ♡
